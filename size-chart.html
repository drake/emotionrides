Approximate — measure a hoodie you like

Lay a tee or hoodie you already wear flat. Measure pit to pit (chest) and collar to hem (length). Compare those numbers to the chart. These are garment measurements in inches, not body measurements. Small shop — cuts can vary a bit by blank.

Hoodies run oversized. If you want fitted, size down. Questions: sbackemoto@gmail.com.
