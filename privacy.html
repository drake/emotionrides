Privacy

Small shop. Short policy. I don’t sell your data.

What I collect

  • Drop list. If you join “Get the drop,” your email goes to our list provider (Resend) so we can send drop alerts and rare codes. That’s it for the list — unsubscribe anytime, or email sbackemoto@gmail.com.
  • Orders. When you buy on shop.emotionrides.com, Big Cartel and Stripe handle name, email, shipping address, and payment. I see what I need to pack the order. I don’t store card numbers.

What I don’t do

I don’t sell your data. I don’t run a pile of marketing pixels. This site is static HTML. The YouTube embeds are YouTube — their player, their cookies.

How long

Order info stays as long as I need it to ship, handle a return, or answer you. Drop-list emails stay until you ask off. Email sbackemoto@gmail.com and I’ll take you off.
